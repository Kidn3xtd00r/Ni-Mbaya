How do leagues work?
  • Every Monday you're put in a league of 15 people at your level, from all over Kenya.
  • You earn XP for check-ins (+20), quests (+10 to +50), beating a craving on the panic button (+50) and helping your group.
  • Sunday midnight: top 5 move up a league, bottom 3 drop down one.
  • XP never comes from spending money. You can't buy your way up. That's the whole point.
Why these names?

Kenya's ride ladder: the handcart, the motorbike taxi, the minibus, the party bus (the one with the lights and the sound system), then the express train. Same destination, faster every league.
