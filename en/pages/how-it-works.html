How it works

A slip is a day, not the end.

Most trackers drop you to zero after one slip, and that's exactly when people give up. Bead by Bead keeps every day you earned in plain sight, and helps you take the next step.

01

The daily loop.

Under a minute a day.

  1. Morning: pledge"Just for today, I won't smoke." One tap. Today
  2. All day: your planYour reason and your "when… I will…" plan for the hard moment. Your plan
  3. Any time: SOSBreathing, your reason, and ten-minute ways through a craving. SOS
  4. Evening: check inKept, slipped, or need a pause. All three are fine answers if they're true. Check in
  5. Watch it growOne bead per kept day, in the flag's colours, and the shield every 7th day in a row. Your bracelet
02

Our promises.

Honest beads

A bead only means a day you kept. A pause gets a knot, never a bead. A missed check-in is never counted as a slip.

Nothing taken away

A slip ends a streak, not your progress. Every bead you earned stays on the bracelet.

No shame

We say "slip", never "fail" or "relapse". Malebo jokes about the weed, never about you.

No tricks

No coins, no spins, no mystery prizes, no buying your way to a streak.

Private and discreet

No name needed. When saving arrives, your record stays on your phone only. The app's name and icon never say "weed".

Real help

An app isn't treatment. NACADA's helpline is free, 24/7 and confidential: 1192.

03

Where this comes from.

What we built on, and what we're still testing.

  • The slip moment. Relapse research describes the "abstinence violation effect": after one lapse, people feel they've failed completely and give up. The slip screen is built to interrupt that.
  • Withdrawal. Cannabis withdrawal usually starts in days 1 to 3, peaks around days 2 to 6 and fades within about 2 weeks: Budney and others, 2003.
  • The bracelet. The flag's colours and the Maasai shield and spears, which the flag's makers took from Maasai culture: WorldAtlas.
  • Still a test. We don't yet know whether the bracelet helps people keep going. That's what the first version is for.
