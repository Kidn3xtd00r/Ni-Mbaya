How it works

They gamified your habits. We gamified your comeback.

Betting apps, loan apps and "just one" are all built to keep you coming back. Bead by Bead uses the same tricks (streaks, XP, leagues) to pull you the other way.

01

The daily loop.

Thirty seconds a day. That's the whole commitment.

  1. Check in

    "Hey! How did today go?" Tell Malebo how today went. +20 XP, and the streak grows. Check in →

  2. Do your quests

    Four small missions a day, like turning down one "buy me a drink". Each one pays XP. See today's quests →

  3. Climb the league

    Your XP puts you on a weekly leaderboard with 14 Kenyans. Top 5 move up. See the league →

  4. Hit panic, not relapse

    Craving? Driver, drop me here! Beat it and you earn +50 XP. Panic button →

  5. Give the money a job

    Everything your habit used to eat goes to your goal: fees, rent, your group, home. Savings →

02

The game, word by word.

Everything you'll see in the app, in one list.

Streak
Days in a row you've checked in clean. Every one of them adds a bead.
Streak Saver
Insurance for one missed day. For life (a funeral, a trip, no network), not for relapses.
XP
Points for everything good you do. XP sets your level and your league place.
Coins
Game coins from quests. Spend them in the shop. Never real money, never for sale.
Leagues
Weekly leagues: Handcart, Motorbike, Minibus, Party Bus, Express Train.
Habits
What you're quitting: betting, alcohol, Fuliza debt, 3am scrolling. "What's your habit?" is Malebo's favourite question.
Beads
The heart of the game. One bead for every clean day, in the Kenyan flag's colours, with a shield charm every 7th. 30 beads make a bracelet, 100 a necklace, 365 the Hero's Collar. Beads are never taken away.
Savings group
Your small squad. Shared quests, a shared savings pot, and alerts when someone's struggling.
03

Fair play.

The rules we hold ourselves to.

  • No pay-to-win

    You can't buy XP, coins or a streak. Ever. Discipline is the only currency.

  • No slot-machine tricks

    No spins, no mystery boxes, no "surprise" prizes. Random rewards work exactly like a slot machine, and if you're quitting betting you don't need another one in your pocket.

  • No betting ads

    Not one. Not "responsibly". Not for any amount of money.

  • We roast the habit, never you

    The jokes go at the habit, not the person. Slips are logged as lessons, and nobody gets humiliated.

  • Streaks that forgive

    Streak Savers, a pause button for exams or a funeral, and a consistency score, so one bad day isn't zero.

  • Real help, when it's serious

    Struggling with alcohol or drugs? Call 1192: NACADA's free, 24/7 helpline. For betting or debt, talk to someone you trust. XP is not therapy.

★

Why it's built like this.

Not vibes. Psychology. (Okay, also vibes.)

Loss aversion
Losing a streak hurts about twice as much as growing it feels good. That's why streaks work. It's also why rigid ones make people anxious and quit, so ours come with Streak Savers and a pause button.
A face beats a bar
People look after a character more than a progress bar. That's why Malebo has moods, and why your progress is a bracelet you can see growing, not just a number.
Humour lowers shame
Recovery research finds humour helps people cope and fights self-stigma. Shame does the opposite. So Malebo jokes, but never at you.
Choose, grow, belong
Motivation lasts when you get to choose (tone, nag level, what Malebo calls you), see yourself get better (levels, beads, the jewellery box) and feel part of something (your group).
No random prizes
Habit apps often use surprise rewards, the same trick as a slot machine. We keep the surprise in the jokes (a new meme of the day, every day), never in the prizes.
