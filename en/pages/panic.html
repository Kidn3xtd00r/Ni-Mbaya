03

What's normal.

When someone who smoked often stops, their body takes time to adjust. Feeling rough is a sign of change, not failure.

Days 1 to 3
Irritability, restlessness, less appetite and poor sleep often start here.
Days 2 to 6
Usually the hardest stretch. This is what SOS is for.
Up to 2 weeks
Most of it fades. Sleep and strange dreams can take longer to settle.

Source: Budney and others, "The time course and significance of cannabis withdrawal", Journal of Abnormal Psychology, 2003. Everyone is different; if you feel unwell, see a doctor.
