02

How to earn beads.

Real Kenyan bracelet beads, one a day. You can't buy them, you can't rush them. You can only earn them.

One bead for every clean check-in, always in the flag's order: black, white, red, white, green, and round again. Exactly how a Kenyan bracelet is strung.
The shield
Every 7th day in a row, the bead is the Maasai shield from the heart of the flag. The centrepiece of every real Kenyan bracelet.
The gap
A missed day. No bead, and the streak resets. But no bead is ever taken away. Messed up? Fine. We keep going.
Streak Saver
Life happened (a funeral, a trip, no network)? A Streak Saver covers the day and you still get its bead.
