Your data.

What Bead by Bead keeps, where, and how to get rid of it.

  • What's kept: your plan, one record per day (kept, slipped or paused), and any notes you write. No name needed, no phone number, no location.
  • Where: right now, nowhere. This is a prototype and nothing is saved. Later, it will be saved only on this phone.
  • Who can see it: only someone holding your unlocked phone. Use a screen lock.
  • Discreet: the app's name and icon never mention weed.
