01

They gamified your habits. We gamified your comeback.

The apps that drain Kenyan pockets are built like games: streaks, rewards, notifications. Bead by Bead uses the same tricks to pull you the other way.

  • Betting"It's a sure bet." It never is. The house always eats first.
  • Fuliza & loan apps"I'll pay it back Friday." Borrowed today, owed forever.
  • Alcohol"The very last one." You said that last week too.
  • 3am scrolling"Just one more video." Then the alarm goes off. Touch grass, friend.
