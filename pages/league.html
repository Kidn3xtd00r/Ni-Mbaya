How does Ligi work?
  • Every Monday you're put in a league of 15 people at your level, from all over Kenya.
  • You earn XP for check-ins (+20), quests (+10 to +50), beating a craving on the panic button (+50) and helping your chama.
  • Sunday midnight: top 5 move up a league, bottom 3 drop down one.
  • XP never comes from spending money. You can't buy your way up. That's the whole point.
Why matatus?

Mkokoteni (handcart) to Boda to Matatu to Nganya (the matatu with the lights and the sound system) to SGR. Same destination, faster every league.
