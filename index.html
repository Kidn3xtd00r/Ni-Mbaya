01

They gamified your anasa. We gamified your comeback.

The apps that drain Kenyan pockets are built like games: streaks, rewards, notifications. Ni Mbaya! uses the same tricks to pull you the other way.

  • Betting"Ni sure bet." It never is. The house always eats first.
  • Fuliza & loan apps"Nitalipa Friday." Borrowed today, owed forever.
  • Pombe"Ya mwisho kabisaa." You said that last week too.
  • 3am scrolling"Video moja tu." Then the alarm goes off. Touch grass, rafiki.
